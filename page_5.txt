Section 3  |  Proposed Solution
Smart Share connects riders through ride requests. A user signs in, creates a request, and asks the system to
find compatible requests from other users.
  Create a ride request. Choose a pickup and a destination (the current UI offers Mumbai landmark presets
such as BKC, Airport T2, Powai, Lower Parel, Dadar and Andheri West).
 Select a departure window (earliest and latest acceptable departure).
 State seats needed and the maximum extra minutes the rider will accept.
 Find matches. The backend discovers candidates, filters them and evaluates routes.
 Review ranked suggestions with score factors, each rider's estimated detour, the stop order and a
routing-accuracy notice.

How candidates are found: the engine shortlists active, unexpired requests from other users within 2 km of the
pickup, with overlapping departure windows and combined seats of at most four. It then rejects pairs travelling in
clearly different directions, evaluates the valid pickup/drop-off orders using road-network durations, and discards
any order in which a rider's extra time exceeds their own limit. Survivors are scored and the top five are returned.
Scope note. The documentation does not describe automatic cab booking, driver assignment or payments as
implemented, and this report does not claim them.
